How to install mod:
1. Open the zip file called "Mario Mod".
2. Go to the Quake 4 directory located wherever your Steam files are "...\Steam\steamapps\common\Quake 4".
3. Place the folder inside the zip file in the Quake 4 folder.
4. Launch Quake 4 and click the Mods option.
5. Select the Mario Mod and click "Load Mod".

New features added in the mod (So far):
1. Modified gauntlet to punch enemies.
2. Jump three times in quick succession to perform a triple jump.
3. Press forwards and jump at the same time on the ground or in the air to perform a long jump.
4. Hold the space button in midair to perform a ground pound.
5. Jump while crouching to perform a higher jump.
